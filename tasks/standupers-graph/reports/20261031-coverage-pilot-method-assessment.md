## What the yield says about the next method

The 70% floor requires 31 more positive-linked nodes from 39. The completed focal slices added three new degree nodes from five, but that number is not a reliable conversion rate: it mixes one high-yield multi-review archive, a recovered saved context, an island-bridging link, and slices that ended with unused budgets. Repeating isolated exact-name-performer queries is not yet adequate evidence of a route to 70%: Magda and Pięta yielded no resolved pair, Olka’s new source did not add a degree node after correction, and Kamiński’s six additional group reviews did not identify peers. Current evidence is fan-only, so style performance claims cannot fill this gap.

Recommended experimental routes, in order:

1. **Mine full authored fan/review posts across multiple frozen performers, starting from source pages and archive time slices.** Karol’s six substantive KupBilecik time slices yielded nine canonical candidates, eight accepted records and five source-local Karol–Chmiel co-endorsements; this is the clearest observed acquisition yield. Try to capture posts that mention and evaluate several named performers, then verify each identity and preference separately. This route can efficiently add links and may bridge components, but its single-family concentration is a real limitation. **Measure:** unique source-local authors, canonical posts, accepted pair records, newly linked nodes, and separately the changes in corroborated nodes and component sizes per source page/time slice; fail or revise the route if opened pages mostly yield repeats, solo praise, unresolved names, or no accepted pairs.
2. **Repair exact context/identity in already saved source material, with independent review.** The verified batch-1 correction added two linked nodes without new acquisition; the Kamiński–Radek recovery added a provisional pair from a saved post. Pięta’s remaining Dominik event-roster question is a specific saved lead. This is bounded and can proceed offline where saved full text is sufficient, but these gains are finite and must not turn a name guess into an edge. **Measure:** each proposed correction’s source/quote, identity resolution, independent decision, and actual delta to degree, corroboration and largest component; count zero if review rejects or graph recomputation produces no qualifying edge.
3. **Test broader review archives and structured public ratings only after accessibility/provenance checks.** Archive pages can yield many source-local opinions, as Karol shows, but Pięta’s archive slice did not resolve a pair, and Sawczuk archive leads are still search-only. For any structured same-user ratings route, first verify full-page access, identifiable author/post context, and whether the rating directly expresses the relevant individual preference; do not infer it from star scores or roster membership. **Measure:** opened and provenance-complete pages, eligible explicit preferences, resolved pair records, and resulting graph deltas. Stop treating a route as useful if pages remain inaccessible or evidence cannot be tied to a distinct source-local author and individual performer.

The separately approved performance-verified style layer remains conditional on deeper fan research staying below 70%. If used, report its degree and components in a separately labeled layer; do not merge those values into fan-overlap counts.

## Unresolved conceptual risks

- **First names are not people.** Source-local hashes can distinguish commenters only within a source family; a bare “Michał” cannot be joined to another platform identity or a frozen performer without direct context. Retain unknown identities rather than borrowing another author’s context.
- **Group-medium bias.** A reviewer’s appraisal of a cabaret, musical group, or mixed lineup does not establish liking for every named member. The six Kamiński/HrAbi skips demonstrate the practical boundary.
- **Degree coverage hides disconnected islands.** The repaired Olka–Karol pair and Kamiński–Radek pair can make names “connected” while remaining outside the main network. Always report positive degree, corroborated degree, and largest component separately.
- **Archive concentration limits generalization.** Five independent labels on one site are five source-local endorsements for that pair, not cross-domain replication or a representative audience estimate. Track family/site concentration alongside yield.
