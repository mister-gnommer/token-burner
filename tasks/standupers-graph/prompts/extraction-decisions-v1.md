# Extraction decisions v1

Apply alongside extraction and verification prompts; preserve their actual versions in run provenance.

- Use one mention per resolved performer per record, combining all occurrences in the complete excerpt. Keep a representative verbatim surface form; repeated names never multiply fan support.
- Keep programme-specific evaluations scoped in review notes. Criticism of one show does not establish dislike of the performer's entire work. Relative improvement alone does not establish absolute praise. If negative versus mixed remains ambiguous, mark the record uncertain rather than infer appreciation.
- Explicit liking with criticism can support co-endorsement with caveats. Evaluate each pair from the actual text; neutral mentions in the same record do not become endorsed performers. A mixed label alone is insufficient proof of liking.
- An unevaluated out-of-universe metaphor need not become a mention. Retain unresolved relevant performers with null IDs and uncertainty; never invent canonical identities.
- Different fans' style impressions, and claims about attempted imitation, do not establish fan overlap or a factual similarity/dissimilarity relation. Keep their wording and scope separate.

These decisions come from the complete-context calibration and parent adjudication. Confidence numbers are model judgments, not measured probabilities. All calibration is excluded from graph input.
